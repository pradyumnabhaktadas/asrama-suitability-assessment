# Āśrama Suitability Assessment

Nov 8, 2026 · @Pradyumna Bhakta Das

## Purpose and how to use

This form helps a mentor see a young devotee's nature and judge which āśrama suits him: brahmacārī or gṛhastha. It looks at real-life behaviour, not just his stated wish to join the ashram.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Complete Honesty is Essential for Perfect Analysis:**
> For a true and accurate evaluation, the candidate **must answer all questions with complete honesty**. There are no "right", "wrong", or "better" answers—both *brahmacārī* and *gṛhastha* āśramas are completely honourable paths meant for serving Śrī Kṛṣṇa. An idealized or artificially polished answer will only distort spiritual guidance, whereas genuine transparency ensures the devotee is placed in the exact environment where he will thrive joyfully, steadily, and peacefully in devotional service.

- **Who fills it:** the devotee answers Parts A–C alone and honestly. The mentor keeps the scoring key (Part D) and fills Part E after a one-to-one talk.
- **Do not show the scoring key to the devotee.** If he knows which answer "scores" brahmacārī, the result is useless.
- **Tell him first:** "There is no right or wrong answer. Both āśramas are meant for serving Krishna. We only want to understand your nature so you can be happy and steady in devotional service."
- **What it measures:** emotional attachment to parents, dependence on them for decisions, ability to stand firm, inclination toward married life, attachment to comfort and career, tolerance for austerity, fitness for community life, real motive, sense control, self-discipline, and attachment to friends and recognition, follow-through on his aspiration, and hidden dependence. It also records family duties and natural inclination (varṇa), which are not scored but guide the decision.
- **Respect is not dependence.** Loving and respecting parents is good. What the form looks for is whether he can act on his own conviction, without needing their approval for every step.

The form should be filled in voluntarily and privately. It is a guide for counselling. Never use it to shame a devotee, to pressure him to cut off his family, or as an automatic admission decision.

## Part B – Real-life situations (52 questions)

Tick one option for each question. Answer what you actually do today, not what you think you should do. If no option fits, write your own answer beside it.

### 1. Emotional attachment to parents

**Q1.** You are on a 10-day yatra with devotees. How often do you feel the need to call home?
- a) Every day, sometimes several times; I feel restless if I can't
- b) Once every 2–3 days, just to tell them I'm fine
- c) Once or twice in the whole yatra; I'm absorbed in it

**Q2.** Your mother says with tears, "If you join the ashram, who will look after us?" What happens inside you?
- a) I feel compassion but stay clear; I would explain gently and see how they can be cared for
- b) I feel guilty and confused for many days
- c) I drop the idea at once; I cannot see her cry

**Q3.** Imagine living in the ashram and visiting home only 2–3 times a year. How do you feel?
- a) Very difficult; I don't think I can stay away that long
- b) Peaceful; that is enough

### How to read the result

**Deciding areas come first.** Areas 1, 2, 4 and 9 show the core nature. A score of **3 or below in any of them** means the brahmacārī āśrama is not suitable now, whatever the total.

- **Dependent on parents:** low scores in Areas 1 and 2, especially Q6 = a, Q7 = b and Q8 = a together. He consults his parents even on small matters and has never gone against them. He is unlikely to be able to leave home, and if he does join, family pressure will probably pull him back.
- **Gṛhastha nature:** a low score in Area 4 (Q13, Q15 and Q16 most of all). The natural desire for a wife and family is strong. Guide him toward a Krishna-conscious gṛhastha life, which is fully honourable.
- **Weak sense control:** a low score in Area 9, especially Q34 = b or Q36 = a. Brahmacarya is built on this. Joining with this struggle unresolved often ends in a fall, so encourage marriage at the right time rather than forcing renunciation.
- **Unsteady:** a low score in Area 10, especially Q37 = a or Q38 = b. Inspiration without self-discipline fades. Build steady habits for 90 days before any trial stay.
- **Social and recognition attachment:** a low score in Area 11. Often hidden behind good sādhana; watch it during a trial stay.
- **Escapist motive:** Q29 = a, or Q30 = c. He wants to run from a problem, not toward Krishna. Help him solve the problem first.
- **Ashram attraction without devotion:** Q32 = a. The attraction is to the atmosphere, not to sādhana itself.
- **Real family duties:** if the family-duties table shows dependent parents, a sick parent or an unfinished commitment, he may need to wait even with good scores. Do not mistake duty for attachment.
- **Varṇa answers (V1–V3):** mostly a or b (brāhmaṇa or kṣatriya tendency) usually fits full-time ashram service well. Mostly c or d (vaiśya or śūdra tendency) often flourishes in gṛhastha life, serving through work, donation and practical help. Treat this as a hint, not a verdict, and use it to choose his service.
- **Talks but doesn't act:** a low score in Area 12, especially Q46 = a. This is the devotee who keeps saying "I will join" without taking small steps. Don't debate the decision with him. Give him one small, time-bound step (a weekend stay, a 30-day vow) and let what he does show the answer.
- **Hidden dependence:** a low score in Area 13, especially Q49 = a or Q50 = b. It confirms Areas 1 and 2: if he cannot handle disagreement or manage his own daily routine, he is still emotionally dependent on home.
- **Relief on hearing both paths are honourable (Q52 = b):** this often means he was saying "brahmacārī" because of outside expectation, not his own nature. Talk about it gently; it is a release, not a failure.

**Overall band** (only once Areas 1, 2, 4 and 9 are each 4 or more):

|---|---|---|
