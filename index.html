🌸 Purpose and How to Use

Understanding psychological nature, emotional independence, and genuine capacity for devotional stability.

⚠️
Crucial Requirement: Answer Honestly for Perfect Analysis For a true and perfect analysis, you must answer every question with 100% honesty. There are no "right", "wrong", or "better" scores—both brahmacārī and gṛhastha āśramas are equally sacred, honourable paths to serve Śrī Kṛṣṇa. An idealized or artificially polished answer will only distort your spiritual direction, whereas genuine, transparent answers ensure your mentor can guide you into the exact environment where you will remain steady, joyful, and spiritually protected.
💡
Golden Principle: "Both brahmacārī and gṛhastha āśramas are completely honourable paths meant for serving Śrī Kṛṣṇa. We only want to understand your real nature so you can be joyful, steady, and peaceful in devotional life."

This assessment helps a counselor discern a young devotee's real-life nature, rather than an idealized sentiment or external pressure to join or marry.

Key Guidelines:

  • Self-Directed & Private: The devotee answers Parts A, B, and C alone and with complete honesty.
  • Hidden Scoring: The scoring rubric is intentionally kept for the mentor only so candidates answer according to actual habits, not what scores "brahmacārī".
  • Respect vs. Dependence: Loving and respecting parents is a noble virtue. This form measures whether one possesses mature inner conviction to make spiritual decisions without requiring maternal or paternal validation for every step.
  • Compassionate Application: Never use this assessment to shame a devotee, force renunciation, or alienate families.
